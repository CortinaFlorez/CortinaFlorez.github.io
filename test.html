Little Seed Big Dreams

By Cortina Florez

I created the story "Little Seed Bid Dreams" to depict growth and the changes we have to go through to get there. Although it is sometimes hard for us to accept the uncomfortability of change in our own lives, it is so effortlessly beautiful in nature. It seemed like a good parallel for the message I was trying to convey. In life we start off as one person and before we know it we are someone completely new with all new memories and accomplishments to show for it.
